Abstract

Brains are born with largely pre-specified cortical wiring, and the only rule locally available to a synapse is correlation between the neurons it connects; machine-learning agents instead optimise a global objective. How far does the biological combination reach on its own? We built a system of 148,032 model cortical neurons connected by 309,148 pre-specified “instinct” synapses, driving a simulated quadruped with no reward, no error signal, no gradient and no training loop. Placed prone, it stands up on 5/5 network seeds; shown red, it walks toward the stimulus on 5/5, over a median 1.73 m; with no sensory input it collapses; with the instinct table cleared the same brains can neither stand nor approach (0/5). Silencing the prefrontal population abolishes visually guided walking (0/200 ticks, 5/5) while leaving a wired reflex unchanged. The behaviour is produced by the cortex rather than by the stimulus: removing the recurrent step removes the action while sparing the reflex. Adding innate structure grows the repertoire without retraining; a 200-cell dopamine population writes one new sense-to-action link during the life of a single brain; gaze tracking emerges from static look-at rules and a motion-onset reflex rather than being written down. Acquisition becomes a recipe once the sheet is given somewhere to write: a route laid between two populations at a weight too small to do anything, twenty seconds of contact, and one cell whose firing says only that this moment counts - after which a tone that meant nothing drives the retreat cells at 1.000, while the same lesson writes nothing if the route was not laid down or the animal was never touched. The sheet can also be grown rather than retrained: a rebuilt engine carries 584,137 cells and 1,812,331 synapses at 11.2 ms per tick on one consumer GPU, four times the cells of the reference build at about a tenth of the cost. No rule in the system lowers a weight, and the acquired walk does not yet stay upright; both are reported rather than hidden.

What is and is not claimed

Fixed on 18 September 2026: the born walk used to topple at tick 66. The cause was not learning, not the gait chain and not the return line - it was the prefrontal population running away (2608 → 2818 cells and still rising), so that it lit, alongside “red ahead”, the cells for “red to the left”. The instinct table carries both “prefrontal: red ahead → walk forward” and “prefrontal: red to the left → turn left”, so two actions were ignited on every tick; the two muscle force trains summed and the body tilted from the first tick. Cutting the return line's branch into the motor area changes nothing; cutting the prefrontal–motor-memory edges removes walking entirely. With the prefrontal population held sparse by dynamic global inhibition - now the default - the same brain walks 600 ticks / 6.62 m without ever tilting past 75°, and the paper's ablations still hold. Evidence: docs/.

Same day — the prefrontal bundle is now complete. The prefrontal population used to receive only three paths (visual / auditory / motor). It now receives seven, adding proprioception, balance, body state and motor memory, and the reciprocal return line is sliced back along the very same list (one table, one source of truth). Sensory regions get no return line: a body reading can only be reported by the body itself, so an imagined tilt must not actually be corrected — with a return line into those three regions the prefrontal echo lights 12–22 cells there, mis-fires “stand up” / “stand”, and the body topples at tick 126. After the change the ablations still hold: prefrontal dark → 0/200 ticks; black screen → 0/200; standing with red ahead for 5 s → 2.82 m, never tilting past 75°, with exactly one motor-memory moment lit throughout. Evidence: docs/.

Reported as a gap, not hidden: the acquired walk does not stay upright for long. Five taught seeds all fall eventually; in the test phase, some seeds topple between tick 45 and tick 101. The cause is narrowed down in Section 4.6 - the current the prefrontal population sends to the fifteen gait cells lands exactly on the gait chain’s firing threshold (0.850), so a drift in the ongoing thought can knock the chain out. Damping that one inborn link fixes one seed and breaks the other three, so it was not applied.

Reported as a gap, not hidden: no rule in the system ever lowers a weight. The three decay settings that were tried make the model fall earlier, not later.

The paper also lists the experiments whose outcome contradicted the prediction (Section 4.5), and says for every number which script produced it and where the log is.

How it learns, and how big it can get (27 September 2026)

Two questions the earlier results could not answer: where does a new capability get written, and does the sheet scale? Both are now measured (Sections R12 and R13 of the manuscript). Every number below has a named log in this repository.

A lesson. A route between two populations is laid down at birth at a weight too small to move any cell - 0.0001, floor 0, ceiling 1.5, and no pull-back towards its birth weight. A keeper then has a hand on the body for one second and off for one second, twenty times, while a 262 Hz tone plays. Nothing innate carries that tone to the retreat cells: at birth the tone moves them by 0.000. After twenty seconds the tone alone drives them at 1.000 and the leg-drive cells at 0.504, with the hand gone.

The rule knows only that two cells fired together. It has no representation of having been right, and the arm that is never touched shows what that means: the route falls back to exactly zero while the tone plays just as often.

Growing it. Nothing in the architecture refers to the number of cells. A rebuilt engine carries 584,137 cells and 1,812,331 synapses and runs the whole loop - eyes, brain, physics - in 11.2 ms (89 steps/s) on one consumer GPU, against 158 ms a tick for the 148,032-cell reference build: four times the cells at about a fourteenth of the cost. What caps a bigger sheet is two build rules, not the hardware - one region's own incoming excitation budget, and one number in the arena file that decides how wide the eyes may be drawn. Cells are cheap; connections are what a tick costs.

Reported as zero, not hidden: a “something is coming at me” cell exists in this build and reads 0.000 in every scene we can build, including a ball closing from 3 m to 0.35 m at 3 m/s.

What the middle layers are for, what a bigger sheet costs, and the eyes and ears (28 September 2026)

Four measurements added to the manuscript as R14–R17, with two new supplementary figures. Every number has a named script and a kept log in engine_v2/.

The middle layers keep what the last layer throws away. In a small case built for it, two positions drive the compressed last layer to exactly the same value, so a read-out on that layer cannot tell them apart at all; with a plastic route from the intermediate cells, two motor cells separate and each answers its own position. On the body, stimulus B then A then B moves the trunk to 0.250, 0.147 and 0.250 m - the second B recovers the first to within 6.8 micrometres. Switch learning off and the separation does not appear. A separate instance runs ten minutes with learning always on and holds it.

A tick costs what is lit, not how many cells exist. With the external drive held fixed, 100,000 to 800,000 cells cost 1.28 to 1.35 ms a step: eight times the cells for 1.06 times the time. The older engine costs 5.17–5.71 ms whatever is alight, from 0 to 86,064 cells - it was never sparse. And “the model is too small” is measurable rather than an opinion: with ten motor cells per channel, forces of 0.50, 0.51, 0.52 and 0.54 all produce the same pattern; with a hundred, the step is 0.01.

The sheet is spatial. Three arms of 1,000 cells that differ only in their coordinates: two objects that share x and y and differ only in depth overlap by 0.084 on a solid sheet against 0.290 on a flat one and 0.343 with random wiring. The cost is honest too - the flat sheet keeps two unrelated objects slightly further apart (0.041 against 0.081).

Eyes and ears. The eyes converge, focus and move to wherever the picture changes, with none of the three written down; the ears tell front from back and left from right from two raw waveforms, with no spectrum, delay estimate or angle computed anywhere outside the cells. Reported as unsolved: the left eye always turns further than the right, the eyes come to rest about 0.1 rad to one side of a ball, and convergence falls to zero past about a metre because the stereo offset is written in pixels rather than in degrees. Two earlier readings got worse when the eyes were made honest: the distance reading from 2.50 m had been bought by distorting the picture, and the pinna cue for front/back was a delay that the left/right circuit was reading as the other ear.

Check any number yourself

Every figure in the paper is produced by a named script and leaves a log. The repository carries all 292 logs and the scripts that wrote them.

cd model
python 核对_论文数字.py        # reads logs/ and compares every number against the paper (~seconds)
python 核对_论文数字.py 重跑   # actually re-runs R1 and checks the live result (~6 min)
python 看_大脑开车.py          # drives the robot again and regenerates the playback page

The two results of 27 September 2026 come from the rebuilt engine in engine_v2/, whose scripts, tests and logs are published beside the reference model; R1–R11 need only model/ and logs/.

Core dependencies: Python 3 + NumPy. The body uses MuJoCo; the figures use matplotlib + Pillow. Everything runs on CPU. See docs/复现说明.md for the per-result table (R1–R11, forty-odd scripts).

中文摘要

大脑一出生就带着大量已经接好的皮层连接，而一个突触在局部能用的规则，只有“一起亮过的细胞之间连接变强”。机器学习走的是另一条路：优化一个全局目标。 这两样东西只靠生物的配方，能走多远？本文建了一个 148,032 个模型皮层神经元、309,148 根出生就写好的“本能”突触的系统，驱动一具仿真四足身体， 没有奖励函数、没有误差信号、没有梯度、没有训练循环。结果：搁着放下，5/5 个种子自己站起来；给看红色，5/5 个种子朝红色走过去（中位数 1.73 米）； 完全不给感觉输入，身体瘻下；把本能表清空，同样的脑子既站不起来也走不了（0/5）；把前额叶那群细胞静默，视觉引导的行走直接消失（0/200 拍，5/5）， 而本能反射丝毫不受影响。 一个 200 细胞的多巴肝群，在一个脑子的一生里写下一条全新的“感觉→动作”连接；眼神跟踪是从静态的“盯着看”规则和运动起始反射里自己长出来的。 系统里没有任何一条规则会把权重调低；学会的走路还不能长时间保持直立。新增（2026-09-27）：两块脑区之间可以先铺一层出生就有、但权重小到不传导的“空连接”，然后用二十秒时间一边放那个声音一边摸它，声音就从“毫无意义”变成“命令后退”——只放声音、撤掉手以后，后退细胞仍然是 1.000；同样放二十秒声音但从不摸它，那条连接掉回 0.000，什么都学不到；没铺空连接的话，同样的一课也什么都不会留下。规则只知道“这两个细胞一起亮过”，它并不知道自己“对不对”。

另一件是规模：架构本身不涉及细胞数量，重建后的引擎有 58 万 4 千个细胞、181 万根连接，把眼睛、大脑、物理一整套算一步只要 11.2 毫秒（每秒 89 步），而原来的 14.8 万细胞版本一步要 158 毫秒。限制做大的是两条自己定的构建规则，不是显卡。这两件事都写在论文里，没有藏。
